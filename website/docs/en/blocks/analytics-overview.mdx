Use this block at the top of a dashboard to show a few key numbers and how they changed. Each card
pairs a value and its change with a small trend line, and the chart below shows weekly revenue in
detail.

This is a copy-ready composition, not an `AnalyticsOverview` component exported by moduix. It
combines `Card`, `Badge`, and `Chart` with TanStack Charts. Install the block with its
dependencies, then replace the sample numbers with data from your application.

## Customize the block

Replace `metrics` and `revenue` with data from your API. Keep `value` and `change` as
formatted strings so each card shows exactly what you pass in, and give `sparkline()` the raw numbers
for the trend.

The trend lines are decorative: they have no axes, tooltip, pointer, or keyboard focus, and each one
keeps a short `ariaLabel`. Put the exact numbers in the card text, not only in the line. The
revenue chart uses `scaleUtc` for dates and `Intl` formatters: compact amounts on the axis and exact
amounts in the tooltip. Change the locale and currency in these formatters to match your product.

Colors come from `chartColors`, so they follow the active theme. See the [Chart](/docs/chart),
[Card](/docs/card), and [Badge](/docs/badge) docs for the composed parts.

Drawer: `DrawerContent` accepts `size` (`sm`, `md`, `lg`, `xl`; default `md`) that sets the width of `start` and `end` drawers to 18rem, 22rem, 28rem, or 36rem, still capped at `100vw - 2rem`. It is mirrored as `data-size`. Top and bottom drawers are unchanged. The `DrawerSize` type is exported.
